## Scientific Claims Compliance

### What This Manuscript DOES Claim

1. **Formal system completeness:** A minimal, independent axiom system for CIIR
   is presented; all primitives are formally defined; all theorems are proved.

2. **Theoretical framework:** CIIR provides a Hilbert-space operator-algebraic
   framework for bounded cognitive systems, derived from first principles.

3. **Mathematical rigor:** The framework meets the standards of graduate-level
   mathematical physics; a mathematician can directly extend or critique it.

4. **Falsifiability:** Three quantitatively falsifiable predictions are derived
   as testable inequalities with explicit falsification criteria.

5. **Generality:** Classical probability, Bayesian inference, and quantum
   cognition are formally embedded as limiting cases.

### What This Manuscript DOES NOT Claim

1. ❌ CIIR provides a complete physical theory of consciousness or sentience.
2. ❌ The framework supersedes quantum mechanics or classical probability.
3. ❌ Experimental validation of the predictions has been completed.
4. ❌ The axiom system is the unique minimal basis for any theory of cognition.
5. ❌ Gödel incompleteness is overcome (the system is relatively complete
      within its stated scope).
