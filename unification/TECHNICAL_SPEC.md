## Simulation Engine Scaling

- CIIR strict executor: O(N_constraints) per step
- MVRI loop: O(N_steps × |State|²) for entropy/MI computation
- Plasma RMHD: O(N_grid²) per timestep
- Multi-qubit density matrix: O(2^N × 2^N) for N qubits
